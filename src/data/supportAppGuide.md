# Diress — user guide for the support assistant (25 Sep 2026)

Plain-language description of what users can see and do in the Diress mobile app. Written from the user's point of view: screens, buttons and steps — no technical details. Numbers not listed here (exact prices, credit amounts per tool) must not be invented; point users to the screen that shows them.

## What Diress is
Diress turns a seller's product photos into professional e-commerce visuals: model photos, clean product photos, marketplace listing sets, banners and short product videos. It works for clothing and also for general products (shoes, jewelry, bags, cosmetics, home goods, food and more).

## Navigation
- Bottom bar: **Home**, **History**, **Settings**.
- Top of Home: credit balance (tap to buy credits or see plans), "Get PRO" / trial badge with time left, "N processing" counter while work is running.
- **Home, from top:** before/after showcase · quick tabs (Popular, Enhance, Editing, Remove BG) · **AI Fashion Studio** card · **Listing Image Studio** card · **Image to Video** card · **Marketplace tools** grid (with **Create your own tool** next to the section title). Tapping a tool card first shows a short intro with before/after examples, then **Continue**.
- **History (Creation History):** all creations; filters for images, videos and kits; albums (long-press → Add to Album); Download All; delete items; share an album link.
- **Settings:** sign in / sign up · membership status and credits (Team vs You when in a team) · Team management · Dark mode · Language · Notifications on/off · Support (Contact Us, Feedback, Rate the app) · Privacy Policy, Terms · Log out · Delete my account (cannot be undone). Restore purchases is on the plans (paywall) screen: "Restore".

## Main tools
**AI Fashion Studio** (Home card) opens: See it on a model · Change pose · Change color · Back side · Change model · Change location · Film looks · Banner templates.

- **See it on a model:** upload the product (camera, gallery or earlier creations), single item or whole outfit. Choose a location (ready places, favorites, or describe your own venue), a model (ready models, your saved models, or "Let AI decide"), gender and age, optional color change, advanced options (pose, hair, skin tone, camera angle…) and an optional detail note. Styles include Standard, Editorial, Street Style and Close-up; jewelry has its own options, including a product-only shot. You can also upload a reference photo of a shoot you like (its place, light and pose are used) or save "My Shoot Styles" from brand photos. Pick the number of images, aspect ratio and resolution in the bottom bar, then Generate. Tip: add front, back and close-up photos; keep a hand, body or mannequin in the photo so the size comes out right.
- **Change pose:** upload a model photo, then pick a pose for each result — a ready pose, your own custom pose, or **Let AI decide** (the AI picks the best pose for the setting, outfit and concept). Picking a pose is optional.
- **Change color:** pick a color or type a Pantone code; bulk mode handles many photos at once.
- **Back side:** add the garment's front and back photos to get a back-view shot.
- **Change model / Change location:** start from an earlier creation or a photo of the product being worn; the outfit stays the same, only the model or the place changes.
- **Product Retouch** (marketplace tools): turns an amateur product photo into a professional one — flat lay, ghost mannequin, color lineup, angles for shoes/eyewear/jewelry, or "clean my photo as it is"; choose background (including a color), shadow and reflection. Downloads include transparent PNG.
- **Marketplace tools / Product Studio:** task cards such as Place in a Scene, Hand Holding Product, Product in Action, Detail Shots, Packaging & Label Mockup, Design Mockup, Seasonal Campaign, Jewelry Mode, Food and Beverage shots, AI Image Expand and more. Each screen: upload a product photo, set the options, optional note, choose ratio and number of images.
- **Create your own tool:** describe the tool you want in a paragraph (optionally add before/after examples), review the summary, then create it. It is prepared in the background and appears first in Marketplace tools; you can rename or delete it. Up to 3 new tools per day.
- **Listing Image Studio:** upload a product, choose the marketplace (or your own website) and which images you need (main, features, lifestyle, with model, comparison, dimensions, close-ups, how to use, what's in the box, or your own), add product details (photos or a PDF work too), choose a style, then Download all.
- **Banner Studio:** make sale/campaign/new season/brand banners with your prices, code and deadline, in the language you choose; static image or animated.
- **Banner templates:** swipe ready templates by category and place your product photo in them. **Change the content** rewrites all the text from your topic, offer or code; **Match colors to the outfit** (on by default) adjusts the accent colors to your product. Hold to see the original template; save or share.
- **Image to Video:** photo-to-video for products (showcase, in use, unboxing, macro details, 360° spin, on a model…), choose scene, camera, platform/ratio, duration (5, 8 or 10 seconds) and quality (720p or 1080p; 1080p costs extra credits, shown in the menu). Videos take a few minutes. The video studio also has sales videos, UGC-style videos, close-ups, 360° videos and commercials.
- **Film looks:** film-camera style filters for a photo (instant, 35 mm, flash, disposable, black & white and more) with strength, paper texture, note and date stamp. Free.
- **Enhance (upscale):** increase resolution; higher resolutions cost more credits.
- **Remove BG:** free background removal, also in bulk, transparent PNG.
- **Editing room:** quick edit ideas (hair, makeup, outfit, background, light, accessories, expression, season) or your own request. Many rounds of edits on the same image can slowly reduce quality.

## Image viewer (tap any result)
Download (format options JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF; optional "AI generated" label) and Share · tools: Edit (AI edit chat), Back pose, Different pose, Change color, Remove background, Banner, Compare before/after, Text, Save as model · **Diversify** for more poses/variations · **Kits** (e-commerce, real-life, unboxing sets) · Credit refund · Report.
- **Edit (AI edit chat):** describe the change and optionally select an area; editing here is free. You don't have to wait on the screen: leave, and the result appears in the bubble on the left edge.

## While things are processing
- You can leave the screen. For creating a model, creating a location and AI edits, a round bubble appears on the left edge of the screen — it spins while working and shows a check when ready; tap to open. The bubble can be dragged.
- In tool screens, when you scroll away from Results, a small progress bubble appears above the scroll arrow; tap it to jump back to Results.
- A notification ("Your process is complete!") can be sent when a creation finishes; notifications can be turned off in Settings.

## Credits, PRO and trial
- Creations use credits; the amount depends on the tool, number of images and resolution. Some things are free (Remove BG, Film looks, downloading a banner template as it is, AI edit chat).
- **PRO subscription:** credits are added every billing period, no watermark, high-quality downloads and video tools.
- **Free trial:** generation is limited, downloads carry a watermark and some features (such as video) may be limited. When the trial converts to a paid plan, the plan's credits are added and the watermark is removed.
- **Credit packs:** one-time purchases, not a subscription. Buying any credit pack also removes the watermark.
- **Teams:** members share the team leader's credits; only the team leader can buy credits; members are invited by email in Settings > Team (the person needs a Diress account first); extra member seats can be bought there.
- If a creation fails, the credits spent on it are returned automatically ("No credits were used" / "Your credits were refunded").

## Credit refund for a bad result
- Where: open the main result in the viewer and use **Credit refund**. The button shows its status; when a refund isn't possible it looks red — tap it to see why.
- What counts: the product came out clearly different, an important product detail was lost or invented, obvious anatomy errors (e.g. extra/missing limbs), or a broken/corrupted image. Personal taste, pose, style, background or small shade differences do not count.
- Who/what is eligible: main creations that keep your product as it is (model photos, jewelry, Product Retouch, back side, pose change), within **24 hours** of creating, not during the free trial. Not eligible: kit and variation images, color change and AI-edit results, and images changed in the edit screen (ask for the refund before editing). Each image is reviewed once. Reviewing is free; a refund returns the credits spent on that creation.
- If the automatic review says no, you can **request a manual review** once and describe what went wrong. If the team approves it, you get a notification and a "Your refund is approved" screen explaining why; you are not notified about rejections, but the result shows in the refund sheet.
- Money refunds for store purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not inside the app.

## Common problems
- Not enough credits → buy a credit pack or a plan (team members: ask the team leader).
- Photo too large or unreadable → choose another photo (clear, well-lit, whole product visible).
- Saving to the gallery doesn't work → allow Photos/Media permission for Diress in the phone settings.
- Restore purchases says nothing found → make sure you're signed in with the same store account; "receipt already in use" means it belongs to another Diress account (contact support).
- Some content (e.g. lingerie, swimwear) can only use an AI-chosen model; very sensitive content may be blocked by safety filters.
- "Wait for your current generations to finish" → too many jobs at once; try again when they're done.
- Anything else, or account-specific issues → Settings > Support > **Contact Us** (the team replies by email).
